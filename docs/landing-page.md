# PickleAI — Landing Page Plan

Goal: convince a pickleball player in ~20 seconds that this will make them better, and
get them to sign up / start a free analysis.

**Primary CTA everywhere:** *"Analyze my first clip free"*

## Section-by-section

### 1. Hero
- **Headline:** *"Your personal pickleball coach. In your pocket."*
- **Subhead:** *"Upload a clip of your game. Get pro-level feedback, drills, and a
  plan to improve — in minutes, not $100/hour."*
- **CTA button:** *Analyze my first clip free* → sign-up.
- **Visual:** phone/laptop mockup showing a video frame with pose overlay + a feedback
  card ("Open your paddle face later on the drop"). This visual does most of the selling.
- Trust strip below: *"No coach required · Works with any phone video · Cancel anytime."*

### 2. The problem (relatable)
- Short, punchy: *"You practice. You play. But you keep making the same mistakes —
  and you can't see why. A coach could tell you… if you could afford one every week."*

### 3. How it works (3 steps)
1. **Upload** — Drop in a clip from your phone.
2. **We analyze** — Our AI breaks down your technique, positioning, and shot selection.
3. **You improve** — Get prioritized fixes and drills, and track progress over time.
- Each step: icon + one line. Keep it skimmable.

### 4. What you get (feature/benefit cards)
- **Frame-by-frame technique breakdown** — see exactly what your body is doing.
- **Prioritized fixes** — the 3 things that'll help most, ranked.
- **Real drills** — specific practice, not vague advice.
- **Progress tracking** — watch your weak spots turn into strengths.
- **Pickleball-specific** — dinks, third-shot drops, kitchen positioning, stacking.
  (Emphasize: *built for pickleball, not a generic sports app.*)

### 5. Sample analysis (show, don't tell)
- An embedded example report: annotated frame + the actual coaching text + a drill.
- This is the highest-converting section — let them *see* the output quality.

### 6. Social proof (as it accrues)
- Testimonials, rating ("went from 3.0 to 3.5 in a season"), player count.
- Placeholder-ready for launch; fill in as reviews come.

### 7. Pricing
- Clean tier table (see `monetization.md`). Highlight the recommended plan.
- Emphasize: *cheaper than one lesson a month · cancel anytime.*
- CTA on each tier.

### 8. FAQ
- What video works? (Any phone; ideally side/back angle, whole point visible.)
- How accurate is it? (What it's good at, honest about limits.)
- Do I need special equipment? (No.)
- Is my video private? (Yes — private by default, delete anytime.)
- Can it replace a coach? (Complements; feedback whenever you want, far cheaper.)
- What skill levels? (Beginner to advanced.)

### 9. Final CTA banner
- *"Stop guessing. Start improving."* → *Analyze my first clip free.*

### Footer
- Links: pricing, FAQ, privacy, terms, contact, login. Social handles.

## Design direction
- Energetic but clean. Sporty accent color (pickleball yellow-green + a deep contrast).
- Mobile-first — most players will hit this from a phone.
- Fast load, real product visuals over stock photos.

## Conversion mechanics
- One dominant CTA repeated (don't dilute).
- Free first analysis = the hook; ask for payment *after* they've felt the value.
- Capture email early (even pre-analysis) for follow-up nurture.
